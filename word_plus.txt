[Words Plus+]

동사 + -ed: 감정 동사 → 감정 형용사  
excite → excited  
worry → worried  
surprise → surprised  

Kids are excited in the pool.  
She is worried about the test.  
I was surprised by the gift.  

접미사 -less: 일반 명사 + -less (~이 없는) → 형용사  
hope → hopeless  
care → careless  

Our project is hopeless.  
She is a careless driver.  


[유의어]
finally = lastly  
present = gift  
sick = ill  

[반의어]
agree ↔ disagree  
lose ↔ win  
turn off ↔ turn on  

[다의어 동사]
hold (개최하다; 잡다)  
lose (잃어버리다; 지다)  
miss (놓치다; 그리워하다)  
work (작동하다; 일하다)
