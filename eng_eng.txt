agree (동의하다): to have the same idea or feel the same way  
attitude (태도): the way you think or feel about something  
fair (박람회): an event where people go to buy or see things  
famous (유명한): known by many people  
fault (잘못): responsibility for a problem, mistake, bad situation, etc.  
finish (끝내다): to reach the end of something  
hopeless (가망 없는, 절망적인): being no hope that it will get better  
kid (농담하다, 놀리다; 어린이): to say something as a joke, not seriously; a young person  
learn (배우다): to get to know through study or experience  
lose (경기에서 지다): to fail to win a game, contest, etc.  
miss (놓치다): to fail to do, get, or catch  
nervous (긴장한, 초조한): feeling worried or anxious  
positive (긍정적인): thinking something good will happen  
present (선물): something given as a gift  
quit (그만두다): to stop doing something  
speech (연설): a talk in front of people  
support (지지, 응원): the act of giving help or encouragement to someone  
ticket (표, 티켓): a piece of paper you buy for a show or an event  
waste (낭비하다): to use something foolishly or carelessly  
worried (걱정하는): feeling fear and concern about the future
