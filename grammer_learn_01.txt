[핵심 문법]

1. be동사

I am Jihun, and my best friend is Minsu.
You are strong.
We are not good players.
Is Mina a good student?
Are they teachers, too?

be동사는 '~이다', '(~에) 있다'의 의미를 가지며,
주어에 따라 am, are, is로 변한다.

I → am
You → are
He/She/It → is
We/You/They → are

I'm a doctor.
He is my brother.
They're at the cafe.

2. be동사의 부정문

be동사 + not

I am not in the library.
She is not a teacher.
We are not hungry.

3. be동사의 의문문

Be동사 + 주어?

Are they good friends?
Yes, they are. / No, they aren't.


[일반동사]

I play the guitar, and Minsu plays the drums.
They have three cats.
She doesn't talk much.
Do you walk to school?
Does Jiho get up early?

일반동사는 be동사를 제외한 대부분의 동사로, 주어의 동작이나 상태를 나타낸다.

1. 주어가 3인칭 단수일 때 일반동사의 변화

대부분의 동사 + -s
talks, likes, reads, gets, smiles, feels, sleeps

-s, -o, -x, -sh, -ch로 끝나는 동사 + -es
passes, brushes, does, goes, fixes, catches

자음 + y → y를 i로 바꾸고 + -es
cry → cries, fly → flies, study → studies, try → tries

불규칙 변화
have → has

He goes swimming every Saturday.
Mr. Smith studies science.
He has a new book.

2. 일반동사의 부정문

주어 + do not(don't) + 동사원형
주어가 3인칭 단수 → does not(doesn't)

I do not play basketball.
She doesn't like pizza.

3. 일반동사의 의문문

Do/Does + 주어 + 동사원형?

Yes, 주어 + do/does.
No, 주어 + don't/doesn't.

Does he play tennis?
Yes, he does. / No, he doesn't.
